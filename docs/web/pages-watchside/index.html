How it works

Presence, then a door.

Watchside does one thing: it tells you where people are, and gets you there in one press.

1

See where your friends are

Add a few people who also use Watchside. When they start watching someone, they show up in your panel with the channel they are on.

2

Notice when people gather

Friends watching the same stream are grouped together, so three people landing on one channel is something you can actually see.

3

Jump in with them

Press JOIN and Watchside takes you to that stream. No lobby, no invite to accept, no waiting for anyone to reply.

Why it matters

Twitch is better when you know where your people are.

Watching alone and watching together are different experiences, and the only thing standing between them is usually not knowing.

Someone is already here

When a friend is on the stream you are on, Watchside says so. What was a solo tab quietly becomes a shared one — and the friends who are elsewhere are one press away.

Watch it together

Everyone who joined shares a small side conversation and quick reactions, next to the stream rather than on top of it. Twitch chat carries on exactly as it was.

Your data

It knows where you are watching. That is all.

Watchside is built to show your friends one thing about you, and it is the thing you already chose to do in public.

  • Runs on Twitch only. It never reads Twitch chat, scrapes the page, or runs on any other site.
  • No ads, no trackers, no selling. No advertising SDK, no third-party analytics, no fingerprinting. This website sets no cookies.
  • Your password stays with Twitch. Sign-in goes through Twitch's own page. Watchside never sees it.
  • You can go invisible, or leave. Turn presence off whenever you like, and delete your account and its data at any time.

The privacy policy says exactly what is kept, for how long, and how to delete it.
