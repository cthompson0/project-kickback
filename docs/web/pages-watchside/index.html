watchside

See where your friends are watching Twitch.

Watchside is a browser extension that shows you which streams your friends are on, so you can jump in and watch together.

How it works

  • Add a few friends who also use Watchside.
  • When one of them starts watching someone on Twitch, they appear in your Watchside panel.
  • Press JOIN and you land on that stream, alongside them.

Watchside sits beside Twitch in a small panel. It does not replace Twitch chat and it does not change how you watch.

Get Watchside

Add to Chrome

Watchside is in a small private beta. A Firefox version is built and working, and is waiting on Mozilla's review before it can be listed — there is nothing to install there yet.

Your data

Watchside shows your friends where you are watching, and nothing else. It never reads Twitch chat, never touches other sites, and never sells or shares anything. The privacy policy says exactly what is kept, for how long, and how to delete it.
